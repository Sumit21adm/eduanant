Cloud-grade software. Hosted inside your school.

Most school software stops working the second the internet cuts out. EduAnant installs directly on your school's local PC or server, and staff reach it instantly over your own Wi-Fi router — even when the broadband line is down. Student records and financial ledgers never leave campus, so data ownership is never in question. Nightly local backups run automatically.

Sound familiar?
"Fee disputes at the counter."
A parent arrives with an old receipt insisting last quarter was paid. The clerk spends twenty minutes in a physical ledger while a queue builds.
With EduAnant: search the student's name and the full history — dues, advance adjustments, split cash/UPI entries — is on screen in under 30 seconds. Reprint a branded PDF receipt on the spot.
"Twenty minutes gone in first period."
Roll call on paper registers, late-comers overwriting entries, and a register that has to be totalled by hand at month end.
With EduAnant: one-tap bulk attendance in under a minute on any phone or laptop. If a teacher is on leave, the system prompts for a substitute immediately.
"No record of who came and went."
An illegible paper visitor book, and no reliable trace of which student left early or with whom.
With EduAnant: a dedicated Reception Counter module logs visitors, phone enquiries and digital student gate passes — searchable months later.
