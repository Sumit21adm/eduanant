This page is the rule for every brochure, card, slide, invoice and sign we produce. If a piece disagrees with this page, the piece is wrong. Everything in docs/print is generated from it.

The mark
{{MARK}}
An infinity drawn as an open book, wearing a graduation cap. Anant means endless. Use on white and on any light wash.
{{MARK_WHITE}}
The white mark is the only one permitted on navy. The gradient version loses its left loop against a dark ground.
The lockup — what actually gets printed
The mark never appears alone on paper. On screen the name is in the address bar and the page title; on paper there is nothing to tell a principal what we are called. A director who likes the leaflet has to be able to ask for us by name, so the printed logo is always mark + name, in one of the three forms below.
{{LOCKUP}}
Primary
Default for every piece. Masthead, covers, letterhead, invoices.
{{LOCKUP_DARK}}
Reversed
On navy panels, slide covers and the back of the visiting card.
{{LOCKUP_HI}}
Bilingual
Hindi pieces, and anywhere the reader has never met the word “EduAnant”.
Clear space & minimum size
{{LOCKUP_SM}}
Keep clear on all four sides by the height of the graduation cap. Nothing — no rule, no photo edge, no other logo — enters that band. Minimum printed height of the mark: 7 mm. Below that the cap tassel and the page lines of the book fill in on press and it reads as a grey blob.
Never do this
Never substitute a letter
The first brochure used a navy square with an “E” in it because the real logo was not to hand. We have a logo.
Never use public/logo.png
An older, differently coloured rendering that survives in the repo. Retire it. The SVGs are the only approved artwork.
Never recolour, outline, rotate or stretch
Scale proportionally only. No drop shadows, no glow, no box around it.
Never write the name any other way
It is EduAnant — one word, capital E, capital A, no space, no hyphen. Not Eduanant, EDUANANT, Edu Anant or Edu-Anant. In Devanagari: एडुअनंत.

Rules
Navy is authority, amber is action, cyan belongs to the mark. Amber text on white must use #B45309, never #F59E0B — at 9 pt the brighter amber fails contrast and vanishes under a reading lamp. Do not introduce a fifth colour; if something needs to stand out, give it space, not a new hue.

How we write
  • Say what it does, not what it is. “Mark a whole class in 60 seconds”, not “attendance module”.
  • Rupees, not adjectives. Print the number.
  • Never promise what is not shipped. Every claim traces to src/data/modules.ts or to running code.
  • Hindi is written, not translated. Proofed by a native speaker before press.
