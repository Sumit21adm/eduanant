Sound familiar?
“Fee disputes at the counter.”
A parent arrives with an old receipt insisting last quarter was paid. The clerk works through a physical ledger while a queue builds behind them.
With EduAnant: search the student's name and the full history — dues, advance adjustments, split cash and UPI entries — is on screen in seconds. Reprint a branded PDF receipt on the spot.
“Chasing the same parents every month.”
Someone has to telephone families about overdue fees, and nobody has time, so the dues list grows quietly until the term ends.
With EduAnant: five reminders go to the parent's phone around every due date — three days before, the day before, on the day, the day after, and a week later. Nobody at school lifts a finger.
“Results take a fortnight to reach parents.”
Marks are totalled by hand, grades worked out on a calculator, report cards typed, printed and sent home in school bags.
With EduAnant: teachers enter marks on a phone; grades, GPA and class rank calculate themselves. Parents see the result — and whether it improved on the last exam — the moment it is published.
