Sound familiar?
“Fee disputes at the counter.”
A parent insists last quarter was paid. The clerk works through a ledger while a queue builds. Now: search the name — dues, advances, split cash and UPI entries, all on screen in seconds. Reprint the receipt on the spot.
“Chasing the same parents every month.”
Somebody has to telephone families about overdue fees, and nobody has time. Now: five reminders reach the parent's phone around every due date. Nobody at school lifts a finger.
“Twenty minutes gone in first period.”
Roll call on paper, late-comers overwriting entries, a register totalled by hand at month end. Now: a whole class marked in 60 seconds on any phone — and the system refuses to mark a Sunday or a holiday.
“Results take a fortnight to reach parents.”
Marks totalled by hand, grades on a calculator, report cards sent home in school bags. Now: teachers enter marks on a phone; grades, GPA and rank calculate themselves and publish straight to the parent app.
The part nobody else does
Most school software gives your staff more screens to fill in. EduAnant takes work away from them. These happen on their own, with nobody at school involved: fee reminders around every due date · absence and low-attendance alerts · results published the moment they are ready · homework and morning reminders that know the school calendar · a full backup before every software update.
