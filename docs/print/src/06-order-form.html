What we are committing to.

These terms are part of the order on page 1. They are written to be read by a school, not by a lawyer. If anything here is unclear, ask before you sign — we would rather answer it now.

1. What the fee covers

All {{MODULE_COUNT}} modules, both portals, the Android app and unlimited staff accounts. There is no tier to upgrade to and no per-module charge. Setup, migration from your existing records and on-site staff training are included at no cost. Every future release is included for as long as the licence runs.

2. The pilot, and how you leave

Your first term is a pilot. If at the end of it you decide EduAnant is not right for your school, tell us in writing and the licence ends there — you pay for the term you used and nothing further. We will hand you a full export of your data within 7 working days, in standard formats you can open without us.

3. Your data is yours

Where EduAnant runs on your own computer or network, the database never leaves your building. Where we host it, it stays within India. We do not sell, share or train anything on your students' data. You may export all of it at any time, for any reason, including after you stop paying us.

4. Founding {{FOUNDING_COUNT}} terms

If the Founding {{FOUNDING_COUNT}} box on page 1 is ticked: you pay {{FOUNDING_PCT}}% of the licence for your first year, and the rate you renew at will not rise for as long as you keep renewing without a break. In return you agree to take a reference call from other schools, to give us one quotable sentence after two months of use, and to let us name your school on our website. Group, government and referral rates do not stack with this.

5. What we will do, and when

We will install and configure the system, migrate your records and reconcile them against your own registers before go-live, train each team on their own screens, and stay reachable through your first fee cycle. We answer by phone and WhatsApp on working days. If something is broken and stopping your office working, we treat it as urgent and say so honestly if we cannot fix it the same day.

6. What we need from the school

A computer that meets the specification we give you after the site survey, with a UPS or inverter so a power cut does not interrupt the fee counter. Wi-Fi that reaches the office and the fee counter. Your existing student, staff and fee records in whatever form they are in. One named person at the school who can decide things during the rollout. Verified phone numbers for parents — the parent app is only as good as the numbers it has.

7. Renewal and price changes

The licence runs for the session on page 1 and renews for the next unless either of us says otherwise 30 days before it ends. Billing follows your actual roll at the start of each session, so a school that grows pays more and a school that shrinks pays less. Outside the Founding {{FOUNDING_COUNT}} terms, we will give 60 days' notice of any rate change.

8. Limits, stated plainly

We are a small company and we would rather tell you that than imply otherwise. Support is from the people who build the software. We back up daily and automatically, and we take a full backup before every update — but the school should also keep a copy of the exports we provide. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under Indian law.
